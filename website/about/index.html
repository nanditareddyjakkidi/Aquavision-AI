HOW AQUAVISION WORKS

One image.
A more useful perspective.

Follow the image from its original state to a reviewable environmental screening report.

01

Upload an underwater image

Begin with the original RGB photograph. Preserve the source so every subsequent result can be compared with it.

INPUT
02

Enhance with FUnIE-GAN

The enhancement model aims to improve color, contrast and visibility. It cannot guarantee recovery of information lost during capture.

ENHANCEMENT
03

Detect with RF-DETR

The detector locates visible debris and assigns labels from its trained categories. Bounding boxes and confidence scores make predictions inspectable.

DETECTION
04

Review the observations

Compare the original and enhanced views. Examine labels, object counts and uncertain predictions in their visual context.

INTERPRETATION
05

Read the screening report

Defined rules connect observations to potential environmental concerns and review priority. Unknown material and toxicity remain unknown.

REPORT
06

Download the results

Once analysis is connected, retain the processed image and report for further review.

OUTPUT
Open detection
